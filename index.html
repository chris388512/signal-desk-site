A list of companies that match your ICP is not the same as a list of companies that are ready to buy right now. Firmographics tell you who could buy. Signals tell you who just changed something. You want the second list.

Three steps. You do one of them, once.

  1. 1

    You define the ICP. 15 minutes, one time.

    Industry, headcount range, geography, the job titles you sell to, the technologies or triggers that matter, and anything you want excluded. Plain English is fine — "US B2B SaaS, 20–200 people, that just hired a first RevOps person" is a valid answer.

  2. 2

    We read public job postings against that ICP, every week.

    • Hiring for a specific technology — multiple open roles naming a tool you sell around. Budget is already committed.
    • Tech-stack added — a platform showing up in their postings that wasn't there last week
    • Tech-stack dropped — a tool that disappeared. Often the strongest trigger: someone just churned off a vendor and has a hole.

    Sourced from the companies' own public job boards. Every row links to the actual posting, so you can verify any signal in one click.

    The sheet lands in your inbox Monday morning.

    One Google Sheet link, per-company rows, with the signal, the evidence, and verified contacts. Copy it, paste it into whatever you send from, go to work. Nothing to log into, no seat to assign.
