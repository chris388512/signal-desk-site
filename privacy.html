Privacy notice

Davis Enterprises, trading as Signal Desk. Last updated 16 August 2026.

The short version

We read public job postings to work out which companies just committed budget to something our subscribers sell. We identify a small number of relevant people at those companies from business contact databases, and we deliver that to the subscriber in a spreadsheet. We do not send email on anyone's behalf, we do not sell data onward, and we delete contact details after 180 days.

What we collect, and from where

  • Signal data — job postings published openly on company career pages and public applicant-tracking systems. No logins, no private sources.
  • Business contact data — name, job title, employer, and work email address, obtained from third-party B2B data providers. This is professional contact information, not personal or consumer data.
  • Subscriber data — the email address and targeting preferences (the ICP) you give us when you subscribe or request a sample.

Why we are allowed to process it

For business contacts included in a subscriber's list, we rely on legitimate interest: a documented assessment that a named professional at a company showing a relevant buying signal has a reasonable expectation of being contacted about that need, balanced against their rights. A legitimate-interest assessment is documented before any contact based in the UK or EU is included. For subscribers, we process data to perform our contract with you.

How long we keep it

Delivered rows are retained for 180 days, after which contact fields are purged from our records and from delivered spreadsheets. Signal evidence (the job posting URL and date) may be retained longer, because it is public information about a company rather than about a person.

Your rights

If you are an individual whose work contact details appeared in one of our lists, you can ask us to confirm that, to correct it, or to erase it. Email chris388512@gmail.com and we will action it within 30 days. Erasure requests are honoured using a suppression list of hashed identifiers, so the same record cannot reappear the next time we refresh our data. If you are in the UK or EU you also have the right to object to processing and to complain to your data protection authority.

Who we share it with

Our data providers and infrastructure suppliers, only to the extent needed to build and deliver the lists. Payments, when payment is switched on, are processed by Stripe; we never see or store full card details. We do not sell personal data.

What we will not do

  • We do not send outreach on a subscriber's behalf. You send your own email, from your own systems, under your own sending reputation and legal responsibility.
  • We do not scrape private or logged-in sources.
  • We do not buy or resell consumer data.
