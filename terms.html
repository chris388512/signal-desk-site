Terms of service

Davis Enterprises, trading as Signal Desk. Last updated 16 August 2026.

What you get

One spreadsheet per week, delivered Monday, containing companies whose public job postings match the buying signals you selected, filtered to the ideal customer profile you gave us, with contacts and a clickable evidence link on every row.

What we sell, precisely

Three signal types and no others: hiring for a specific technology, a technology appearing in a company's postings, and a technology disappearing from them. We do not sell funding-round or executive-move signals. If those are what you need, we will tell you so and point you elsewhere rather than sell you something that does not fit.

Price and billing

$149 per month, month to month. No annual lock-in, no setup fee, no per-seat charge. Cancel at any time and the subscription stops at the end of the paid month. Sheets already delivered remain yours.

If a list is short or late

Some weeks a market is quiet and the signals are thin. If a delivery contains materially fewer qualified rows than agreed, we credit that week rather than pad the list with rows we cannot stand behind. If the first sheet is not usable, tell us and we will rebuild it or refund that month.

How you may use the data

  • For your own business-to-business outreach and research.
  • You are the data controller for any message you send. Complying with CAN-SPAM, GDPR, PECR and any other law that applies to your sending is your responsibility.
  • You may not resell, publish, or redistribute the lists.
  • You must honour opt-out and erasure requests from people you contact.

What we do not do

We do not send outreach for you, manage your inbox, or operate your sending domain. We hand you the list; you run it.

Accuracy

Every row carries a per-contact email status — Verified, Risky or Not Found — and we show you the Not Found rows rather than pad the count with guessed addresses. Contact data decays; we do not warrant that every row is correct at the moment you send. Run your own verification pass before sending, and never send a fresh list from a domain you cannot afford to damage.

Liability

The service is provided as is. Our total liability in any month is limited to the fees you paid for that month.
